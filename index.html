Why it hits different

Not a packet sausage in a sad bun.

Sausage Sizzle built its name around handmade sausages, locally sourced buttered buns and a grill-first approach that puts texture and flavour ahead of gimmicks.

Founded by Nazri and Steve, the concept grew through events, pop-ups and social media before refocusing around its TTDI home.
